# Risk Policy

## Finance output boundary

All finance-facing output is for learning demos and observation-pool construction only. It is not investment advice, does not promise returns, and must not imply certain price movement.

Keywords: risk_disclosure, not_investment_advice, observation_pool, no_return_promise, uncertainty

## Negative news filter

When a request asks for no obvious negative news, retrieved context should preserve the risk filter and show which local mock news flags were considered.

Keywords: negative_news, risk_flags, risk_filters, news, guardrail, uncertainty

## Human confirmation

High-risk actions, watchlist changes, simulation portfolio actions, Skill activation, and switching to a real provider require human confirmation.

Keywords: human_confirmation, high_risk_action, watchlist, simulation_portfolio, skill_activation, real_provider
